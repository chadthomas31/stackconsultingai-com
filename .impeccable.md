## Design Context

### Users
Small business owners and operators in Southern California / Orange County who need AI consulting, web development, or business automation. They're typically non-technical decision-makers evaluating whether to invest in better tech. They arrive via search or referral, scanning quickly for credibility and relevance before deciding to reach out.

### Brand Personality
**Bold, Innovative, Fast.** Stack Consulting AI is a cutting-edge builder who moves quickly and ships real results. The voice is confident and direct — no corporate fluff, no hand-holding. It speaks like someone who builds things, not someone who writes about building things.

### Emotional Goals
- **Confidence & Trust** — visitors should immediately feel they're dealing with someone who knows what they're doing
- **Innovation & Edge** — the site should signal forward-thinking, modern tech expertise that's ahead of the curve

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Dark, sleek, developer-focused minimalism inspired by Vercel and Linear
- **Theme:** Dark mode only. Deep navy-black backgrounds (`hsl(222.2, 84%, 4.9%)`), emerald green primary (`hsl(142.1, 76.2%, 36.3%)`)
- **Typography:** Inter — clean, modern, highly legible
- **Motion:** Purposeful animations (fade-in-up entrances, subtle floating orbs, pulse-glow on CTAs). Never decorative-only — motion should guide attention or signal interactivity
- **Surfaces:** Glass-blur effects on navbar, subtle grid patterns, radial gradients for depth. Cards use the dark card token with border separation
- **Icons:** Lucide React — consistent stroke-based iconography
- **Anti-references:** Avoid anything that looks like a generic WordPress theme, stock-photo-heavy corporate sites, or overly playful/rounded SaaS designs. No pastel palettes, no cartoon illustrations, no busy layouts

### Design Principles

1. **Ship signal, not noise.** Every element earns its place. If it doesn't build trust, demonstrate capability, or drive action — cut it.

2. **Dark and sharp.** The dark theme isn't decorative — it's the brand. Maintain high contrast, use emerald green sparingly as an accent to draw the eye to what matters (CTAs, key stats, interactive elements).

3. **Speed is felt.** Performance and perceived speed matter. Animations should be fast (< 700ms), transitions smooth, and page weight minimal. The site should feel as fast as the team it represents.

4. **Technical credibility through craft.** The site itself is the portfolio. Clean code, polished micro-interactions, and attention to detail signal the quality of work Stack delivers to clients.

5. **Clarity over cleverness.** Copy and layout should be immediately scannable. Small business owners don't have time to decode abstract messaging. Lead with outcomes, not jargon.

### Accessibility
- WCAG AA compliance: minimum 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI components
- Full keyboard navigation support
- Semantic HTML with proper ARIA labels
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animations

### Token System
CSS custom properties (shadcn/ui pattern) defined in `app/globals.css`:
- `--primary`: Emerald green accent
- `--background` / `--foreground`: Dark/light text pair
- `--card`, `--secondary`, `--muted`, `--accent`: Surface and text hierarchy
- `--border`, `--input`, `--ring`: Interactive element tokens
- `--radius`: 0.5rem base border radius
