## What NOT to submit

- Do NOT use a different phone number than `(949) 749-0001`. Ever. Not on any listing.
- Do NOT add UTM params to the website URL field — directories often strip query strings, and Google can read inconsistent URLs as different sites.
- Do NOT publish home address. Use city-only + "service area business" toggle.
- Do NOT submit to pay-for-placement directories with DA <20 — they hurt more than help.
